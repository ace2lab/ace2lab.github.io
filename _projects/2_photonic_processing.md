## Ultrafast Photonic Nanomanufacturing

Conventional thermal annealing often requires hours in high-temperature furnaces, which damages flexible polymer substrates and induces undesirable phase segregation. ACE² Lab pioneeers **Intense Pulsed Light (IPL)** flash sintering and photonic nanomanufacturing as a room-temperature, millisecond-scale alternative.

### Key Research Topics

1. **Intense Pulsed Light (IPL) Processing**:
   - Broad-spectrum xenon flash lamp delivering high-energy optical pulses (microsecond to millisecond durations).
   - Photothermal conversion inducing instantaneous surface temperatures exceeding 800 °C while maintaining ambient bulk substrate temperature.

2. **Colloidal Quantum Dot (CQD) Solids**:
   - Suppression and healing of thermally induced surface traps in quantum dot thin films via pulsed photonic sintering.
   - Significant enhancement of carrier mobility, charge extraction efficiency, and optoelectronic device longevity.

3. **Low-Temperature Processing on Flexible Polymers**:
   - Roll-to-roll compatible sintering of metal nanoparticles, conductive metal oxides, and 2D materials directly on PET, PI, and paper substrates.
