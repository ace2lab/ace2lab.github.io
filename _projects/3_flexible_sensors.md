## Flexible Sensors & Environmental Devices

ACE² Lab designs flexible, wearable, and environmental sensors capable of real-time biomonitoring, chemical gas detection, and toxic emission control. By leveraging micro-nanostructured carbon materials and resonator platforms, we develop highly sensitive, selective, and robust sensing architectures.

### Key Research Topics

1. **Laser-Induced Graphene (LIG) Sensors**:
   - Direct laser writing of 3D porous graphene on commercial polyimide films without requiring masks or vacuum environments.
   - Ultra-rapid and highly flexible humidity sensors optimized for continuous human respiration and breath-pattern monitoring.

2. **Microresonator Chemical Sensing Arrays**:
   - Direct integration of Metal-Organic Frameworks (MOFs) and functional porous polymers on microcantilever and microresonator surfaces.
   - Sub-ppm chemical vapor discrimination and volatile organic compound (VOC) monitoring for environmental health.

3. **Environmental Catalysis & Emission Control**:
   - Advanced sorbent regeneration and industrial plant desulfurization technology for PM2.5/fine dust reduction.
